Honeycomb Hustle

Claim a cell by solving its math problem. First team to build an unbroken path across the hive wins.

How to play
  • Honey connects the top and bottom edges. Clover connects the left and right edges. The colored borders show each team's edges.
  • On your turn, tap any open hexagon. A problem appears. Pick the right answer to fill that cell with your color.
  • Miss it and the cell stays open, so anyone can try it later. Then the turn passes.
  • Block the other team while you build. Paths can bend and zigzag as long as the cells touch.
  • Use Topic to focus on fractions or geometry, or keep All topics for a mix of number sense, fractions and geometry. Grade and topic changes apply to the next problem, so you can switch mid-game.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a cell and press Enter, then press 1–4 to answer.
